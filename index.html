Brand Guidelines · v1

The ConVibe Brand System

Conventions · Celebrities · Fans — June 2026

Brand Essence

A backstage pass to the convention universe.

ConVibe tells fans the moment a celebrity they love is appearing at a convention near them. The identity has to feel like the best part of a con — the electric, slightly-overwhelming buzz of a packed show floor under the lights — and make it feel organized and exciting instead of chaotic. The visual world is a cosmic, neon-lit night: a starfield, a glowing convention badge, a gold star. It's playful and gaming-adjacent, not corporate. The feeling we're chasing is the little jolt when you spot a celebrity you didn't know was coming.

Electric

Saturated neon on deep space. The brand glows — it never sits flat or muted.

Playful

Rounded, chunky, gaming-forward. Friendly first; never stiff or enterprise.

Premium-fun

Polished gradients and depth, like a AAA game UI — fun that still feels well-made.

Logo System

Two assets, two jobs.

Primary lockup — badge + wordmark + tagline. Use anywhere ConVibe is named: marketing, splash screen, headers, the web app, decks.
Badge / app icon — the lanyard badge with the gold star on a starfield. This is the App Store icon and the home-screen mark.

Clear space & minimums

Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the badge's star. Don't let other elements crowd it. Minimum legible width for the full lockup is 180 px on screen; below that, switch to the badge-only mark.

Backgrounds

The brand is dark-first — the lockup and badge are designed to sit on the cosmic navy / deep-space gradient. On light surfaces, keep generous padding and avoid busy photos behind the mark. Never place the badge on a clashing saturated color.

Do

✓Use the supplied vector / PNG files at full quality.
✓Place on the cosmic dark gradient whenever possible.
✓Use the badge alone for the app icon, favicons, and tight spaces.
✓Preserve the purple→blue gradient direction of the wordmark.

Don't

✕Recolor, flatten, or rebuild the wordmark in a different font.
✕Stretch, skew, rotate, or add new drop shadows / outlines.
✕Put the badge on a clashing saturated background.
✕Re-rasterize and re-slice the lockup (the May 31 white-seam issue).

Color

Neon on deep space.

The palette is a purple→blue spectrum (the wordmark gradient) lit against cosmic navy, with a single warm gold reserved for the star, highlights, and primary calls-to-action. Gold is the spark — use it sparingly so it always reads as "the good part."

Gradients

The brand moves in gradients, not flats.

linear-gradient(90deg, #7C20C9, #0070E0)

Signature gradient — the ConVibe wordmark. Use for primary buttons, active tabs, key accents. This is the brand gesture.

linear-gradient(180deg, #F5B800, #F58300)

Star gradient — the gold. Reserve for the star, the primary CTA, and "achieved / followed" states. The reward color.

radial cosmic · #1a1150 → #0b0b2e → #04040f

Cosmic background — the starfield base. Behind the badge, splash screens, hero sections, empty states.

linear-gradient(135deg, #B24BF0, #2E9BE6, #5BC5F2)

Glow accent — the badge edge / neon rim light. Use for subtle borders, focus rings, and selected cards.

Typography

Rounded, chunky, friendly.

Logo wordmark
ConVibe

A custom, rounded display face with a heavy 3-D outline — part of the logo artwork, not a system font. Do not re-typeset the wordmark; always use the supplied logo files.

Open item: the exact font used in the lockup wasn't delivered embedded. Get the font name(s) from the Fiverr designer so it can be matched for future marketing pieces.
UI display / headings
Upcoming Conventions

For in-app headings, pick a rounded geometric sans that echoes the wordmark's friendliness — Poppins, Baloo 2, or Nunito. Bold weights, tight tracking. This is where the "gaming UI" feel lives.

Tagline / labels
CONVENTIONS · CELEBRITIES · FANS

All-caps, wide tracking, bold. Mirrors the lockup tagline. Use for section eyebrows, category chips, and small structural labels.

Body / UI text
Wesley Snipes is appearing near you.

Use the platform system font (SF Pro on iOS) or Inter for body and UI copy — maximum legibility and native feel. Keep the personality in the headings; keep the body clean.

App Icon

The badge is the icon.

The lanyard badge + gold star on the cosmic starfield, framed in the iOS rounded square. The starfield fills the icon edge-to-edge so it reads at small sizes; the gold star is the focal point and the one warm element.

Production notes

iExport the master at 1024×1024; iOS generates the rest. Provide all sizes Apple's asset catalog requests.
iBleed the starfield to the edges — Apple applies the rounded-rect mask, so no manual corner rounding and no transparent corners.
iKeep the star and badge centered with safe margin so nothing clips under the mask.
iSource vectors (.svg / .ai) are in the asset folder for clean re-export.

Voice & Tone

An excited friend who knows the floor.

Hyped, not hypey

Genuine fan energy — "Whoa, look who's coming." Never markety or pushy.

Clear & quick

Get the fan from "phone in pocket" to "I'm following them" in the fewest taps. Copy is short.

For insiders & newcomers

Welcoming to brand-new con-goers, but fluent enough to respect the veterans.
